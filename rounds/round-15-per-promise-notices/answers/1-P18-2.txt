## 4. What to say

**Open by naming the gap, don't paper over it.** "We've been quieter than I'd like since August, and I want to use this hour on what matters most to you rather than a retrospective."

**Lead with the compliance track — it's your credibility test.** Bring a dated plan for the security review and audit prep. Don't wait to be asked; arriving with it unprompted is the difference between recovering trust and confirming the pattern.

**Go after the churn signal directly, without using the word.** "Where does Brightline sit in your planning for next year — and what would have to be true for that to be an easy call?" Their answer is the real output of this meeting.

**Probe rate limits and EU customers as one topic.** "Are rate limits constraining what you're doing in the EU?" `DRI-33978` links them; if there's an expansion or residency need behind it, that reframes the account from at-risk to growth.

**Fix ownership on the call.** Commit to being the single point of contact, or name who is. Seven owners in six months is visible from their side too.

**Close by scheduling, not summarizing.** Leave with a dated next touch. The pattern on this account is commitments made and not tracked; break it live.

**Don't do this:** quote metrics, NPS, or ROI. You have none on file. Claiming a number Cobalt can contradict from their own dashboards is the fastest way to lose the room.
