## Short answer: I can't find any record that Advanced analytics was cut — and the current roadmap says it wasn't.

I searched Notion, Slack, Linear, Drive, and the CRM for a cut, deferral, or descoping decision on Advanced analytics. There is no such decision recorded anywhere, and no reasoning or decision-maker to report. Rather than guess at a rationale, here's what the records actually show.

**Advanced analytics is still on the plan.** The authoritative planning doc, **NOT-20876** ("Public roadmap H1 2027", last dated 2026-06-30), lists:

> Advanced analytics: Q2 2027

That's the most recent version of the public roadmap I can find — searching Notion for other public roadmap versions returns only this one. The other ~10 Notion docs titled "Roadmap" (e.g. **NOT-85086**, **NOT-64137**, **NOT-67486**) are one-line stubs with an owner and a topic tag; none contain scope or prioritization decisions.

**The only feature slip announced is a different one.** In **SLA-17908** (#general, 2026-09-19):

> Heads up: Sandbox environments (Rail) slip to Q2 2027. Please update any customers expecting them this year.

That's Sandbox environments, codename Rail — moving from Q4 2026 to Q2 2027. Advanced analytics is codename **Osprey** (per **SLA-93532**), and Osprey appears exactly once across all four systems. It's worth asking whether the "Advanced analytics was cut" message you heard was actually this Rail announcement.
