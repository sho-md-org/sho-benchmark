## What to say

**Open by taking ownership.** "I want to be your single point of contact going forward." It directly addresses the fragmentation, and it costs you nothing if they hadn't noticed.

**Lead with the value arc, not the activity count.** They've moved from deployment questions to reporting and exports — that's a customer getting embedded. Name it as progress.

**Clear the backlog out loud.** Bring the seven open items with owners and dates. Volunteering a stale list you're actively closing builds more credibility than hoping they forget. Prioritize SSO and permissions UI — those read as security/compliance blockers for a clinic network.

**Reopen pricing deliberately.** "We talked commercials in the spring and then it went quiet — where did that land for you?" Better you raise it than have it surface at renewal.

**On churn:** if it's a product request, position it as a roadmap win. If it's internal risk, do not raise the word — instead ask open questions about what's not working and let them tell you.
