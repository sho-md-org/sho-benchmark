## What to say

Given you'll likely go in without hard numbers, lead with the threads rather than metrics:

**Open:** "I want to use today to close the loop on a few things that have been open a while, and get your read on the year ahead."

**Then, in order:**

1. **Own the security/audit thread.** It's the oldest and most likely renewal gate. "Security review and audit prep have been on our list since the spring. I want to give you a firm owner and date today." — Don't let them raise it first.

2. **Ask directly about value, to surface the churn concern.** Don't cite "churn" — you don't know what it meant. Instead: "Where is Brightline clearly earning its keep, and where is it falling short?" The answer tells you whether the flags were real.

3. **Integrations and EU customers as the forward story (DRI-64776).** Newest and most sustained theme — your best expansion angle. "You mentioned EU customers last month — tell me where that's heading."

4. **Let them raise pricing.** It came up 9/10 (DRI-53905) and you don't know the context. If they raise it, listen and take it away rather than negotiating cold.

5. **Close your own item (CRM-62644).** Search has been open since May and is tied to roadmap (DRI-36553). Bring a status, even if it's "not prioritized."

**Avoid:** quoting any NPS or adoption figure, and asserting anything was resolved. Where a thread went quiet — latency, sandbox, exports — ask rather than assume.
